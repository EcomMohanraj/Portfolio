Privacy Policy

TN Bike Race (Android) · Developer: Mohanraj Games, India · Effective 12 October 2026

In short, version 1.0 of TN Bike Race:
  • collects no personal information and needs no account or login;
  • has no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases;
  • keeps your progress and settings only on your phone.

Information we collect

None. TN Bike Race has no account and no sign-in. It does not collect, send or share any personal information, and it does not ask for access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Data saved on your device

The game saves your progress in a file in its private storage on your phone: coins, the bikes you own, paint colours, upgrades, best times, unlocked stages and your settings (graphics quality and sound volumes). This file never leaves your phone. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its storage in Android settings.

Internet access

The game plays fully offline. The Android package lists the internet permission because the Unity engine and Google Play's asset delivery (which installs the game's graphics and sound files together with the app) include it. The game itself sends nothing over the internet.

Third-party services

The game is made with the Unity engine. Unity's analytics, crash reporting, performance reporting, ads and purchasing services are all turned off, so no data is sent to Unity through them.

When you download the game from Google Play, Google handles the download and may collect information under Google's Privacy Policy. We only see the anonymous, aggregated statistics Google Play Console shows every developer, such as install counts, and crash reports that you choose to send to Google.

Children

The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

A future update may add advertising (Google AdMob), which can involve collecting device information such as the advertising ID. If that happens, this policy will be updated with the details, and a new effective date, before that version is released.
