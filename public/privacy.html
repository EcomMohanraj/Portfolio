Privacy Policy

TN Bike Race (Android) · Developer: Mohanraj Games, India · Effective 17 November 2026 (version 1.2)

In short:
  • TN Bike Race needs no account or login, and we (the developer) collect no personal information.
  • Your progress and settings stay on your phone.
  • From version 1.2 the game shows ads from Google AdMob: videos you choose to watch for bonus coins, and an occasional ad between races. Google's ad software collects some device information to show and measure these ads, as described below.
  • There are no in-app purchases and no analytics of our own.

Information we collect

We do not collect, receive or store any personal information. TN Bike Race has no account and no sign-in, and it does not ask for access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show:

  • Reward videos you choose to watch: "Double coins" on the results screen and "Free coins" in the garage (up to five a day). Nothing is shown unless you tap the button.
  • An occasional full-screen ad when you leave the results screen after every third finished race, never during a race, never from the pause menu and never after a practice run.

To show, measure and protect these ads, Google's software automatically collects the following from your device and shares it with Google, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location;
  • interactions with ads and the app, such as app launches, taps and video views;
  • diagnostic information about the performance of the app and the ad software, such as launch time, hang rate and energy use;
  • device identifiers: the Android advertising ID, the app set ID and, if applicable, other identifiers related to accounts signed in on the device.

This data is encrypted in transit. It is collected by Google, not by us; we only see aggregated reports such as how many ads were shown. Google's use of this information is described in Google's Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your consent and choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): on first launch the game shows Google's consent form, where you can choose whether ads may be personalised. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings › Privacy options. This button appears only where the consent rules apply.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Android settings (usually Settings › Privacy › Ads, or Settings › Google › Ads).
  • Watching videos is optional. You can play every stage, buy every bike and upgrade without watching any.
  • Playing offline: the game plays fully without an internet connection. Without one, no ads are loaded or shown.

Data saved on your device

The game saves your progress in a file in its private storage on your phone: coins, the bikes you own, paint colours, upgrades, best times, unlocked stages, your settings (graphics quality, sound volumes, steering and camera) and how many free-coins videos you have watched today. This file never leaves your phone. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its storage in Android settings. Google's consent tool also stores your consent choice on your phone.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load ads (the game itself needs no connection).
  • Advertising ID and Android ad services (AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS): added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK for ads and ad measurement.
  • Wake lock: added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK, so a video ad can play without the screen turning off.
  • Foreground service (data sync): added by Google Play's asset delivery, which installs the game's graphics and sound files together with the app.

Other third-party services

The game is made with the Unity engine. Unity's analytics, crash reporting, performance reporting, ads and purchasing services are all turned off, so no data is sent to Unity through them.

When you download the game from Google Play, Google handles the download and may collect information under Google's Privacy Policy. We only see the anonymous, aggregated statistics Google Play Console shows every developer, such as install counts, and crash reports that you choose to send to Google.

Deleting your data

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to delete. To delete what the game stores on your phone, uninstall it or clear its storage. To limit what Google uses for ads, reset or delete your advertising ID as described above, or use your Google account's privacy controls.

Children

TN Bike Race is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the game's data practices change, this policy will be updated, with a new effective date, before the version with the change is released. Version 1.0 to 1.1 of the game had no ads and collected no data.
